1. Instagram Carousel (5-7 slides): slide 1 = hook, slides 2-5 = value, last slide = CTA
2. X Thread (5-8 tweets): tweet 1 = hook, tweets 2-7 = one insight each, last = CTA + link
3. LinkedIn Post (1500-2000 characters): professional tone, starts with a bold statement, 3-5 insight paragraphs, ends with question to drive comments
4. Instagram Story Series (3-5 stories): story 1 = question/poll, stories 2-4 = teaser content, story 5 = swipe up/link CTA
Output each format as ready-to-post copy with appropriate formatting for the platform.
